Part A · Story 1 · Older people

Older pedestrians carry the heaviest burden

Counts alone would hide this, because age groups differ in size. Per 100,000 people, those aged 75 and over had the most pedestrian bed days in 2021: … against … for all ages, … times as many. Each of their admissions lasted … days. Within every adult age group, pedestrians also stayed 1.7 to 2.7 times longer than car drivers of the same age, so this is not only an age effect.

What can be done

Give slower walkers time to cross. Older adults walk about 0.77 m per second on average, well below the 1.2 m per second that most crossings assume (Western et al., 2025). Longer crossing times, pedestrian detection at signals, and awareness messages aimed at drivers and older people all target this group.
